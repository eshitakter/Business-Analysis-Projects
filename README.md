
# Business Planning & MBR Dashboard (Excel)

A formula-driven Excel model demonstrating annual budgeting, Monthly Business Review (MBR)
reporting, cost variance analysis, profitability analysis, working-capital tracking, and
capital-investment feasibility for a B2B manufacturing business.

## Overview
This project simulates the core analytical deliverables of a Business Planning / FP&A function:
annual budgeting, monthly business reviews, cost variance analysis, product- and customer-level
profitability, cash-conversion-cycle tracking, and a capital-project feasibility case
(NPV / IRR / sensitivity analysis). Built entirely with live Excel formulas — every output
recalculates automatically when the underlying assumptions change.

**Industry context:** modeled on a flexible-packaging manufacturer (foil / film / paper
laminates) supplying large FMCG clients — a representative B2B manufacturing setting for this
kind of financial planning work.

## Data note
All figures in this workbook are **synthetic** — generated with AI assistance and calibrated to
realistic flexible-packaging industry cost and margin benchmarks. They are built solely to
demonstrate analytical method and Excel modeling capability, and do not represent any real
company's disclosed financial results.

## Structure
| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Data_BudgetActual` | Monthly budget vs. actual detail by product line — source data for all other tabs |
| `MBR_Dashboard` | KPI summary + Revenue and gross-margin trend charts |
| `Variance_Analysis` | Cost-category budget vs. actual with variance drivers |
| `Profitability_Analysis` | Contribution margin by product line and by customer segment |
| `Working_Capital_CashFlow` | Inventory/receivables/payables days and cash conversion cycle |
| `Feasibility_NewLine` | 5-year NPV/IRR/payback case for a capacity-expansion investment, with sensitivity analysis |
| `Assumptions_Sources` | Every assumption documented, with benchmark sourcing noted |

## Key findings (from the model)
- Revenue beat budget by 1.5%, but gross margin contracted (32.0% budgeted → 31.1% actual)
- Raw material cost was the largest unfavorable variance (+7.45% vs. budget)
- Distribution & freight had the highest percentage overrun of any category (+17.3%)
- Contribution margin ranged from 31.6% (strongest product line) to 30.3% (weakest)
- Net working capital rose ~35–40% in the peak season, concentrating cash-flow risk
